# Milestone 1

## Phase 1.1 — region and environmental field lattice

Current scope: one static 16×12 hidden lattice; exact matter and volume quanta; three conserved-material channels; real zero-volume solids; deterministic formula initialization; exact probes and conservation audit; V3 sessions and V2 snapshots; four deterministic field chunks; seven-entry environment packages; smooth normal view and optional authoritative raw grid; App/CLI save-load; independent vectors and review evidence.

No field-update algorithm exists in this phase. No organism, cell, genome, metabolism, reaction, diffusion, flow, ecology, or fake-life visualization is present.

## Phase 1.2 boundary

Diffusion, flow/advection, and reactions require explicit new algorithms, conservation/error contracts, tests, and review. They must not be inferred from the Phase 1.1 storage or presentation surfaces.
