# Project Glossary

**Authoritative state** — data required to continue the biological simulation correctly.

**Analytical classification** — a label inferred from observed behavior or history; it does not control biology.

**Biome-like region** — a descriptive grouping derived from conditions and ecological structure, not a modifier package.

**Branch** — a new authoritative future beginning from a snapshot while preserving parent history.

**Cell ancestry** — parent-to-daughter history of individual cells.

**Cheater** — contextual analytical description of an entity receiving shared benefit while contributing less than a relevant reference.

**Cohort** — a future reduced-fidelity representation of many similar organisms with retained distributions and invariants.

**Collective individuality** — the degree to which a group persists, develops, reproduces, controls conflict, and aligns component fate.

**Compiled phenotype** — deterministic resolved functional machinery derived from genotype and ruleset.

**Conservation ledger** — explicit accounting of matter or energy transfer among sources and destinations.

**Cue** — an incidentally informative physical condition or emission.

**Deterministic mode** — supported execution configuration in which the same authoritative inputs produce the same authoritative output.

**Developmental heredity** — inheritance of rules that reconstruct collective or organismal organization.

**Ecological opportunity / niche** — a changing pattern of conditions, resources, interactions, and space under which a lineage may persist.

**Event** — immutable record of a committed transition or analytical detection.

**Explicit simulation** — representation in which individual organisms, fields, and interactions are modeled directly.

**Fidelity contract** — formal statement of what a reduced representation preserves, omits, supports, and approximates.

**Fitness** — retrospective reproductive outcome, not a stored causal currency.

**Genotype ancestry** — parent-to-child history of hereditary architectures.

**Homeostasis** — distributed maintenance of viable internal or organismal conditions.

**Individuality index** — possible analytical summary of higher-order integration; never a causal unlock.

**Intent** — requested biological action before resource and physical resolution.

**Lineage** — historical or analytical ancestry grouping; distinct from exact genotype and species.

**Logical time** — authoritative integer-based simulation time independent of rendering frames.

**Matrix** — persistent extracellular material produced, degraded, and conserved in the environment.

**Natural view** — organic presentation of the living world without scientific overlays dominating.

**Phenotype** — realized biological machinery and behavior produced by genotype, development, state, and environment.

**Presentation snapshot** — immutable read-only projection sent to the rendering application.

**Propagule** — single-cell or multicell reproductive unit that can found a descendant collective or organism.

**Realized action** — action that actually occurs after energy, material, force, and environment constrain an intent.

**Region** — locally continuous habitat with fields, organisms, geometry, connections, and history.

**Rehydration** — conversion of compressed authoritative distributions into a more explicit current representation.

**Replay** — display of retained history; distinct from rerunning the simulation.

**Ruleset** — immutable versioned set of biological primitives, schedules, numeric policies, limits, and algorithms governing a world.

**Scientific overlay** — calibrated visual representation of authoritative, measured, sampled, or inferred data.

**Signal** — a produced physical cue whose receiver response may have evolved consequences for the sender.

**Snapshot** — coherent authoritative world state sufficient to resume or branch.

**Species** — optional operational analytical concept; not a fundamental early engine entity.

**Stigmergy** — coordination through persistent environmental modification.

**World session** — application service coordinating one open world’s simulation, presentation, analytics, and persistence.
