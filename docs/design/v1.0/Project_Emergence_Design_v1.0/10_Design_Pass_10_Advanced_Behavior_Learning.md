# Design Pass 10 — Advanced Behavior, Memory, Learning, and Information Processing

## Central Principle

Evolution changes inherited behavioral architecture across generations. Learning changes an individual during its lifetime through physical modification of its control system.

## Locked Decisions

- Immediate response, lifetime adaptation, and evolutionary change are separate timescales.
- Memory requires physical state, capacity, maintenance, modification, and decay.
- Forgetting can be adaptive.
- Memory forms may include immediate traces, working memory, associative memory, spatial memory, physiological memory, developmental memory, and social memory.
- Habituation and sensitization alter real receptor, threshold, regulatory, or connection state.
- Associative learning changes links among sensory pathways, action pathways, and internal physiological consequences.
- There is no semantic table declaring a signal to mean food or danger.
- Reinforcement-like learning uses internal energy, integrity, homeostatic, resource, stress, or reproductive-condition signals.
- The simulator never supplies reward for approaching a target, defeating an enemy, or earning exploration points.
- Eligibility traces provide bounded delayed credit assignment.
- The genome specifies learning rules, plastic connections, rate, decay, capacity, and reinforcement sensitivity rather than learned content.
- Learning is costly and may be slower, unstable, overgeneralized, manipulated, or maladaptive.
- Fixed inherited responses and learning coexist.
- Organisms may be predisposed to learn some associations more easily.
- Other organisms may exploit habituation, conditioning, reinforcement chemistry, and memory.
- Working memory and recurrent networks permit limited sequences and state.
- Neural-like systems are specialized biological networks, not generic AI agents.
- Primitive cells do not begin with large neural networks.
- Faster communication may evolve through contact relay, excitable membranes, conducting cells, directional paths, and synapse-like interfaces.
- Distributed and centralized control are both valid.
- There is no brain module.
- Morphology can perform computation.
- Reflexes may be inherited and modulated by experience.
- Motor coordination, oscillation, and central-pattern-generator analogues emerge from recurrent control.
- Navigation progresses from local gradient response to path integration, landmarks, and route memory.
- Organisms do not receive maps or coordinates; path integration accumulates error.
- Learning is favored in environments that are variable but structured and repeatedly experienced.
- Developmental learning phases, imprinting analogues, social learning, and cultural-like transmission are possible.
- Cultural information requires actual interaction and can be lost.
- Environment may store external memory through trails, structures, or matrix.
- Internal reinforcement systems can be exploited, producing addiction-like or reward-hacking analogues.
- Pain-like and pleasure-like signals are described functionally without asserting subjective experience.
- The simulator does not automatically claim consciousness, sentience, or self-awareness.
- Intelligence is multidimensional, noncausal, and not an evolutionary destination.
- Planning and world models, if later introduced, must arise from bounded learned internal machinery rather than a hidden search service.
- Exact learned content is not normally written into the genome.
- Save/load preserves activity, memory, and plastic connection changes exactly.
- Advanced behavior remains explainable through sensed input, memory, network activity, action request, and physical constraint.

## Validation Scenarios

- trace decay;
- habituation;
- sensitization;
- forgetting;
- capacity interference;
- cue association;
- extinction and reversal learning;
- nonplastic control;
- internal-consequence reinforcement;
- delayed eligibility;
- mislearning;
- reward manipulation;
- temporary cue loss;
- landmark association;
- route failure after environmental change;
- signal propagation;
- reflex speed;
- bounded recurrent state;
- motor coordination;
- social transmission and cultural loss.

## Deferred Decisions

Exact plasticity equations, network activation, neural cells, synapse interfaces, spatial-memory representation, attention, sleep, consolidation, planning, symbolic communication, and consciousness-language policy remain deferred.

## Acceptance Statement

Learning is valid only when experience changes the organism’s own bounded biological state and thereby alters behavior without any external game objective or hidden agent AI.
