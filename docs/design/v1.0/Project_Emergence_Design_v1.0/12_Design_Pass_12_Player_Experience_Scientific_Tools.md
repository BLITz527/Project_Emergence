# Design Pass 12 — Player Experience, Observation, Experimentation, Intervention, and Scientific Tools

## Experience Structure

Project Emergence supports observer, experimental-scientist, and world-shaper stances within one simulation.

The default experience is observation of an organic living habitat. Scientific depth is progressively revealed through:

```text
look → select → inspect → explain → compare → experiment
```

## Locked Decisions

- Pure observation is a complete experience.
- There is no mandatory win condition, research currency, or complexity goal.
- Optional guided laboratory exercises teach mechanisms without becoming progression gates.
- Navigation spans world, region, ecosystem, organism, cell, internal state, genome, and regulation.
- Selection remains stable across scales.
- Follow mode supports cells, organisms, colonies, propagules, lineages, and fronts.
- The player chooses continuity after division, reproduction, or death.
- Inspectors provide summary, detailed, and expert levels.
- Natural appearance communicates condition before numbers are opened.
- Scientific overlays cover environment, biology, evolution, ecology, collectives, behavior, and conservation.
- Every overlay identifies variable, scale, units, time window, normalization, and uncertainty.
- Time controls include pause, single step, and multiple live simulation speeds.
- Event detection is analytical and may remain provisional.
- Notifications are filterable and nonintrusive.
- Timelines exist for organism, lineage, region, and world.
- Then-and-now comparison is a core tool.
- Causal explanation is a product requirement, with uncertainty and evidence links.
- The standard experiment uses snapshot, control branch, treatment branch, run, comparison, and report.
- Deterministic controls and stochastic replicates are clearly distinguished.
- Laboratory worlds use the same biological rules unless an alternate ruleset is explicit.
- Synthetic genomes are marked permanently as constructed or edited.
- Viability previews identify structural or obvious metabolic concerns, not fitness.
- Environmental, biological, geographic, and time interventions use explicit commands.
- Changing the past creates a branch rather than rewriting history.
- Every intervention enters an immutable ledger.
- Observer and experiment locks prevent accidental contamination.
- A scientific notebook stores observations, hypotheses, links, screenshots, and interpretations.
- Automated suggestions are optional questions, not missions.
- Search and filters use stable IDs and descriptive metadata.
- Lineage trees preserve cell, genotype, colony, organism, and propagule ancestry distinctions.
- Matter-flow, mortality, development, behavior, and relationship networks remain evidence-based.
- Replay distinguishes exact, reconstructed, and summary history.
- Snapshots are authoritative state, not screenshots.
- Branch trees prevent experiment confusion.
- Exported data includes provenance.
- Accessibility includes scalable text, color-independent meaning, high contrast, reduced motion, keyboard navigation, and configurable notifications.
- Scientific tools and software debug tools remain separate.
- Fidelity status is visible.
- Observation does not secretly protect selected entities.
- The interface does not assign moral scores but may warn about irreversible consequences.
- Older worlds retain ruleset identity and are not silently migrated.
- Extinct worlds remain valuable and inspectable.
- Natural, creative, and experimental rulesets are clearly marked.
- The core player loop is notice, wonder, inspect, hypothesize, test, and discover.

## Required Core Views

- cell energy and matter budget;
- why-action trace;
- why-death trace;
- organism development;
- lineage ancestry and range;
- region conditions;
- connection transfer;
- population and mortality timeline;
- interaction network;
- branch comparison;
- experiment report.

## Acceptance Statement

The player experience succeeds when a world is enjoyable to watch without data panels and becomes deeply understandable when the player asks a question.
